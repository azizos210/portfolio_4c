04 — Approach

How I think, end to end.

Three habits that travel with me from one project to the next — secure by design, readable by default.

“Security isn't bolted on at the end — it's shaped into the architecture from the first commit.”

Before writing a line, I trace how data, services and users move through the stack. Most bugs are diagram-shaped.
